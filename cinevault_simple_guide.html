What is Cinevault & How Does It Work?

A simple, non-technical guide explaining what Cinevault is, why we made it, what each part of the app does, and how to talk about it during tomorrow's presentation.

1 What is Cinevault in One Sentence?

The 30-Second Movie Matchmaker
Cinevault is a web app that stops you from wasting 45 minutes scrolling through Netflix, Disney+, and Prime trying to agree on what to watch. Instead of dumping thousands of titles on you, it asks 6 quick questions about your current vibe and time limit, and instantly hands you a tailored shortlist.

2 The Real-Life Problem We Are Solving

The Netflix Scrolling Nightmare
You sit down with friends or family. You open a streaming app. Everyone scrolls through endless rows. Someone says "seen it", someone else says "not in the mood for that". By the time you agree on something, your food is cold or it's time for bed.
Why Current Apps Suck at This
Netflix and Prime recommend things based on what you watched three months ago. But tonight you might be tired, or on a date, or only have 90 minutes. They don't know your current situation. Cinevault does.

3 The 6-Question "Vibe Quiz"

When a user opens Cinevault, they tap through a quick 6-step quiz. Here is what each step asks:

Step 1
Format
Do you want a full Movie, a multi-season TV Show, a short Miniseries, Anime, or a Documentary?
Step 2
Your Mood
Brain-off comfort comedy? Edge-of-seat suspense? Mind-bending sci-fi? Or a dark gritty crime story?
Step 3
Genre Pills
Pick what types of stories you like tonight (Action, Comedy, Horror, Romance, Sci-Fi, etc.).
Step 4
Available Time
Quick 90-minute watch, standard 2-hour movie, or an epic 3-hour marathon? (Features cute clock dials).
Step 5
Who's Watching?
Kids in the room (G/PG only)? Family night (PG-13)? Date night? Or just you (unrestricted R/mature)?
Step 6
Era & Language
New releases (2024–2026), 90s/2000s classics, or international cinema (Korean, Japanese, etc.)?

4 The Result: "Tonight's Feed"

As soon as you tap "Open my feed", Cinevault compiles your answers, searches a massive movie database, and displays ~40 matching titles. You can toggle between Poster Grid View (looks like Netflix) or List View (shows plot summaries, runtimes, and ratings side-by-side).

6 How It Works Under The Hood (In Plain English)

1. Where the movies come from (TMDb)
We don't type movie info by hand. We connect to The Movie Database (TMDb). It's like a giant free Wikipedia for movies that has posters, trailers, ratings, and actors for over 800,000 films and shows.
2. How the quiz talks to the database
When you pick "Kids + Comedy + Under 90 minutes", our code takes those buttons and translates them into a search request that asks TMDb: "Give us the most popular animated movies rated PG that are under 90 minutes."

7 The TV Show Bug We Just Solved (Tell this story!)

A great story for your presentation: "Here is a bug we found and fixed"
The Weird Problem: Earlier, if you picked "TV Show + Action + Kids", the app returned 0 results. But if you switched to "Movie", it showed 180+ results.
Why it happened: In the TMDb database, Movies and TV shows use totally different category ID numbers. For movies, the Action category is number 28. But for TV shows, number 28 doesn't exist — TV calls it "Action & Adventure" and numbers it 10759. Our app was sending the movie number to the TV database, so the database got confused and returned nothing!
How we fixed it: We created a smart translator (TV_GENRE_TO_TMDB_ID) that checks: if the user wants TV, it automatically sends the right TV number (10759). Now it returns over 1,400 TV shows!

8 What Happens to Your Feed When You Click Around?

  • Clicking around the app: If you tap a movie, watch the trailer, and click Back to the feed, everything stays right where you left it.
  • Hard Refreshing (F5) or closing the tab: The vibe quiz is stored in your browser's temporary memory. If you refresh the page completely, the quiz resets back to step 1 and the feed shows the default popular movies.
  • What is permanently saved: Your Watchlist and Watched lists are saved on your phone/computer in localStorage, so they never disappear even if you turn off your laptop!

9 What Tools Built Cinevault? (Simple Tech Stack)

Tool What it is in normal words Why we used it
React 19 & TypeScript The building blocks and programming language. Makes the website super snappy like an app. You never wait for pages to reload when clicking tabs.
Vite The engine that packages the code. Makes the app load in milliseconds on any phone, tablet, or laptop.
Tailwind CSS The design and styling system. Gives Cinevault its dark cinema look (dark gray background, gold buttons, blurred glass menus).
TMDb API The cloud database connection. Gives us real movie posters, release dates, actor lists, and trailer video links for free.

10 What If The Internet Drops? (Offline Backup Mode)

If you're presenting somewhere with terrible Wi-Fi, or if TMDb goes down, Cinevault has an automated Curated Backup Catalog stored right inside the app. It will seamlessly show high-quality verified titles (like Top Gun: Maverick, Avatar, Spirited Away) with zero error messages.
